## Deep cut — Rolling short‑horizon markets that fit our stack
- Instrument: time‑boxed books (e.g., Tennis next 15 minutes: “Any break? YES/NO”; Cricket next over: “≥8 runs?”, “Wicket?”, “Boundary count ≥2?”). Event‑linked, not props. Each settles on official feed prints with our standard challenge window.
- Why it wins for us:
  - LatAm + MENA users engage in session‑style play; short windows recycle bankroll and lift retention vs one long matchline.
  - Our fee reality (240 bps taker, quadratic; maker 0 on‑chain) works better where spreads are tight and turnover is high. Time‑boxed markets concentrate depth; makers can quote simple vol surfaces (P, 1–P) without carrying 2‑hour tail risk.
  - Oracles: Stats Perform/Opta provides ball‑by‑ball (cricket) and point‑by‑point (tennis) with low‑latency official data and live win‑probability models we can benchmark to. ([icc-cricket.com](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/stats-perform-becomes-icc-s-official-data-partner-until-2027?utm_source=openai))
- Market hygiene:
  - Clear voids: abandonments, retirements, rain — void only affected slices; do not touch settled slices. Document per sport in rules.
  - Source of truth: name the specific Opta/RunningBall endpoint and timestamp convention in every market rule. ([developers.statsperform.com](https://developers.statsperform.com/?utm_source=openai))
  - Challenge window: unchanged; publish resolution logs post‑settlement.
- Maker playbook:
  - Quote symmetrical books around implied live probabilities; inventory is naturally bounded by the slice horizon, cutting adverse selection.
  - Off‑chain rebates remain our only lever; pay on filled notional in time‑boxed SKUs to seed depth.

## Competitive (why this differentiates vs peers)
- Polymarket/Limitless: great at evergreen outrights and hourlies; far fewer tightly specified, feed‑anchored micro‑slices in cricket/tennis. Their AMM/pool products carry inventory across long horizons; our CLOB can express sharper micro odds with lower LP capital. ([app.eventgraph.ai](https://app.eventgraph.ai/events?utm_source=openai))
- Kalshi (US DCM): focused on macro, weather, policy, with US surveillance/clearing overhead and recent emergency rule filings around court‑driven edge cases — not a short‑slice sports UX. We should stay out of that blast radius internationally and ship sports where we’re licensed. ([cftc.gov](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules0712269454.pdf?utm_source=openai))
