Title: The spread is a personality test — paying for tight books without touching the on‑chain fee

What we’re solving
- Constraint: taker fee is fixed at 240 bps × P(1−P); maker fee on‑chain = 0. All MM economics must be off‑chain.
- Goal: visible depth at the inside, predictable fills in volatile windows (sports live, short‑horizon crypto), and fewer ‘air‑pockets’ that invite adverse selection.

What good looks like (exchange lens)
- Tight, sticky top‑of‑book in the bins traders actually hit; queue that refills fast after a take.
- Rebate math that rewards displayed liquidity that gets taken (not idle quotes), weighted where fees are largest (mid‑probability).
- Post‑only windows post‑restart and a maker‑friendly last‑look equivalent (on a CLOB that means enforceable post‑only and reject‑on‑cross on restarts, not ‘operator decides’).

How to pay for it (under our fee constraint)
- Off‑chain daily USDC rebates keyed to fee‑curve value of filled maker orders (fee ∝ P(1−P)); minimum payout threshold to reduce dust.
- Category tuning: higher maker rebate where taker fee burden is highest for our users (economics/politics) and lower where we already clear easily.
- Kill wash incentives: exclude matched flow under the same master from rebate eligibility.

Why this wins vs AMMs
- AMMs socialise inventory and price impact; CLOBs price inventory locally and let pros take risk. Our job is to keep the inside thick, the queue fair, and the rebating honest.

Implementation notes
- Metrics to track daily: inside depth at ~10/25/50 bps, refill time after a 95th‑pct take, effective spread paid by small tickets, maker payout concentration.
- Guardrails: venue‑wide self‑match preventers; queue‑jump checks; outage playbook with 2‑minute post‑only after restart.

Competitive references (for mechanism, not hype)
- Polymarket publicly documents a fee‑funded maker rebate that pays out daily in USDC and scales with fee curve value; they keep taker fees symmetric around 50%. Good blueprint for our off‑chain accruals and measurement.

So what for PredictStreet
- Ship a rebate calculator and maker portal; publish category rebate bands; and wire post‑only TIF + reject‑on‑cross into the restart checklist.
