Title: The basis edge we should own

Why this now
- The near-term wedge isn’t another license press release; it’s owning the cross‑venue basis any time our book diverges from Polymarket or AMM venues. Our fee constraint (240 bps quadratic taker; maker 0 on‑chain; MM economics off‑chain) makes this a product + liquidity play, not a pricing toggle.

What to build (MVP in two sprints)
1) Parity feed
- Ingest Polymarket categorical/binary tops of book + last trade into ps‑analytics; normalize to our contract slugs. Flag rule diffs (voids, cutoffs) so false bases don’t page the desk.

2) Basis monitor
- Live delta = PS mid – competitor mid, with confidence from depth and rule‑match score. Thresholds with hysteresis (don’t chase noise). Export to a maker API.

3) Maker protection
- Quote bands for approved MMs: max width, max inventory per outcome, kill‑switch on oracle prints. No preferential on‑chain fee (we can’t); rebates stay off‑chain by account.

4) Auto‑routing UX
- If user is about to cross a wide spread, offer a limit guided by competitor mid (“You improve by 4¢; fills if price returns”). Teaches limits; improves book quality without us warehousing risk.

5) Post‑trade audit
- Basis capture P&L by market; slippage vs target mid; adverse‑selection score around resolution‑adjacent events. Weekly kill/keep list.

Risk controls (table stakes)
- Rules parity check before arming quotes; cooldowns near cutoffs; ban self‑match and rebate farming; log and review same‑master cross‑side fills. 

Why it matters to PS
- Liquidity/MM: Quicker spread collapse → better top‑of‑book → cheaper price discovery for takers.
- Growth: The UX nudge (“improve by X¢”) is sticky, educative, and doesn’t need a promo budget.
- Trust: Clear rule‑diff badges reduce resolution disputes; fewer void surprises.

Next steps (D1–D12)
- D1: Approve read‑only parity scraper scope + legal sign‑off on competitor data use.
- D2: Ship parity feed + monitor (feature‑flagged) in two sprints; wire to an internal sandbox MM.
- D3: Pilot auto‑quoting with 1‑2 trusted MMs on 10 markets; add off‑chain rebate tier for basis provision.
- D4: Roll out limit‑helper UX and resolution rule badges.

Notes for implementation
- Keep data export JSON aligned with emerging EU/MiCA order‑book schemas; it future‑proofs our reporting and makes regulator conversations simpler later.

Callout glossary (1‑liners for the team)
- Spread: ask minus bid — the tax you pay for urgency.
- Depth: size available at each price — your true fill capacity.
- Basis: price difference between venues after fees and rules — either free money or a trap.
- Maker vs taker: posters improve the book; crossers pay the vig (our 240 bps quadratic taker). 
- Adverse selection: getting filled right before the print moves against you.
