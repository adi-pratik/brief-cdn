Private intel — concept issue

Tonight’s playable edge isn’t a headline, it’s a mechanism we control: how our CLOB should manufacture spread, deepen depth, and clip vig without scaring takers. Three parts for product and MM:

1) Turn the CLOB into a factory for good quotes
- Define the spread like a budget: wide enough to pay for adverse selection, tight enough to trade. Our 240 bps quadratic taker fee means the worst fee hit is at 50/50; the edges get cheaper as price moves away from 0.5. So we should list more asymmetric books (60–85% implied) where takers pay less and makers get filled more often. Map this in the UI as a “maker lane” — surface markets where taker fee is sub-200 bps at the inside.
- Off‑chain maker rebates are our only lever; set them to reward time‑at‑touch and queue priority, not just notional. Goal: consistent top-of-book on both sides, not a last‑second snipe.

2) Cross‑venue basis is signal, not trivia
- Run the scanner every hour: PS price vs Polymarket/SX on identical rules. If the basis persists, label it: rules mismatch, oracle cadence, or fee math. Basis that’s fee‑math driven is harvestable; rules mismatch is a trap. In‑app, badge markets with “clean basis” and plot the drift so makers know where to lean.

3) Rails to reduce friction the user can feel
- Where the deposit path is USDC from local real‑time rails (e.g., PIX via partners), the average user sees less timing risk and is willing to cross the spread. That’s worth actual listing space: “Fast settle” tags, and a pre‑trade panel that shows time‑to-funds in minutes, not just networks.

Competitive tells (for context, not headlines)
- Some peer exchanges pay USDC for standing tight, visible as maker‑rewards pools; great for depth, but it can attract wash risk. Read any such program as Trust + Liquidity/MM, not as volume for volume’s sake. ([help.sx.bet](https://help.sx.bet/en/articles/12659744-sx-bet-maker-rewards?utm_source=openai))
- Order‑book hygiene matters when rules clarify mid‑life; on certain venues, clarifications can nuke the book. Our rulebook and challenge window should be explicit about when we clear resting orders, and the UI should pre‑warn. ([help.polymarket.com](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364548-how-are-markets-clarified?utm_source=openai))

What to build next (short list)

- Basis heatmap: flag books with harvestable fee‑math basis; auto‑open maker tickets with suggested two‑sided quotes.
- “Fast settle” catalog: carve a home for USDC‑first rails (including partners who mint/redeem off PIX) with an SLA banner so users know why our markets feel tighter. Why it matters to PredictStreet
- The house doesn’t exist here; inventory does. If we make it cheaper and faster to be filled (fee geometry + rails) and safer to stand size (clear rules on clarifications), we win the boring fight that decides real liquidity: narrower durable spreads, deeper ladders, quieter slippage.
