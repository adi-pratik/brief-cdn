Title — Make the basis your product

Why now
- We still see cross‑venue price gaps on identical (or near‑identical) questions — part fee, part rules, part liquidity timing. That gap is inventory and onboarding if we package it.

What to ship (3 sprints)
1) Basis radar (UI + API)
- Surface per‑market “elsewhere mid” and spread, tagged by venue and rule deltas. Default to offshore CLOB/AMM comps where comparable resolution exists.
- Show implied taker cost head‑to‑head vs us (240 bps quadratic; maker 0 on‑chain), so makers see net edge after fees in one glance.
- API route for MMs to subscribe and auto‑quote our book inside competing mid by a tick when rules align.

2) Maker onboarding kit (off‑chain economics, on‑chain plumbing)
- One‑click synthetic pairs: pre‑templated order ladders sized to our vault risk limits; time‑in‑force + cancel‑replace tuned to event cadence.
- Off‑chain rebate ledger + weekly statements (we can’t alter on‑chain maker 0 bps; all MM economics accrue off‑chain per our constraint).
- “No self‑match” guardrails + alerts to keep wash risk out of rebate eligibility.

3) Rails where users already are (LATAM + India first)
- Checkout modules and docs that make it trivial for partners to accept stablecoins and local rails side‑by‑side — e.g., USDC plus Pix in Brazil now, with UPI previewed by major processors for US merchants. We don’t run fiat ourselves; we integrate the onramp/offramp options our partners expose. [Cites: processor now supports Pix and previews UPI via Link; USDC is widely integrated across chains and partners.] ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026?utm_source=openai))

How we talk about it (to users)
- “Trade the gap” card on market pages: shows basis in cents, our fee, estimated slippage at quoted size, and ‘what you earn if filled’ net of vig.
- Resolution rule diff pill (hover): if rules differ, we show the exact text delta and turn off auto‑quote.

How we talk about it (to makers)
- “Earn the spread, not rebates”: publish transparent MM tiers keyed to time‑at‑touch and depth provided; weekly USDC payout.
- Real examples: publish three recent fills where our quadratic taker fee was lower/worse than AMM decay — teach when to fade vs when to seed.

Risk and controls
- Oracles and voids: only auto‑quote when resolution sources align; otherwise flag N/A and require manual sign‑off.
- Trust: ship live audit trails for MM accruals and a wash‑trade exclusion note in every statement.

Metrics that prove it worked
- Cross‑venue basis half‑life (target: < 30 minutes in top books).
- Maker retention, time‑at‑touch, and depth at the inside for top 50 markets.
- LATAM/India deposit success via partner rails (Pix/UPI where available) and USDC share of funding. ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026?utm_source=openai))

What it does for us
- Turns a spreadsheet headache (basis) into a front‑of‑house reason to trade here.
- Onboards serious makers without promising on‑chain fee changes we can’t make.
- Meets users where they pay — stablecoins globally, Pix/UPI locally — via partners rather than us becoming a payments company.
