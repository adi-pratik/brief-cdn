Title: 20 Aug — Basis and rails: how we win on fees

Tonight’s concept (2–3 min)
- The map is thin; we ship a playbook. Two moves:
  1) Turn cross‑venue basis into P&L by making makers faster and clearer on our CLOB than AMMs can be at the same vig.
  2) Land local rails (PIX/UPI) end‑to‑end for USDC while keeping our 240 bps quadratic taker and maker=0 on‑chain unchanged.

Why this matters now
- The legal wrappers diverge: France just ordered ISPs to block Polymarket offshore; US venues sit under DCM/DCO. We can be the licensed betting‑intermediary in Gibraltar, but our liquidity competes with both. Reading the field accurately is the product edge. Move 1 — Cross‑venue basis into product
- Define basis in‑app: for mirrored markets, surface best external mid, our mid, spread, and implied fee haircut; tag rule deltas that explain persistent gaps.
- Ship maker ergonomics: one‑tap pegged quotes, size‑ladder presets, cancel‑replace hotkeys, and a maker PnL panel that nets off fees and rebates (off‑chain) across legs.
- Guardrails: venue‑rule lints ("resolution source mismatch"), and self‑match prevention that still lets a desk roll inventory up/down the book.
- Proof points: when a DCM lists event contracts (US) and an offshore PM is blocked (FR), the cross‑venue basis often widens around rule/news shocks; being the fastest, clearest CLOB wins that flow. ([anj.fr](https://anj.fr/promotion-dune-offre-de-jeux-dargent-illegale-blocage-du-site-polymarket?utm_source=openai))

Move 2 — Rails without breaking fees
- What to ship: BR PIX and IN UPI as off‑ramp/on‑ramp into USDC custody, abstracted behind our ADI vaults; keep the market fee model flat.
- How: card‑on‑file equivalent via partners; auto‑FX to USDC; compliance screens at deposit; withdrawal flows that map USDC→BRL (PIX) / INR (UPI) when allowed.
- External signal: Stripe’s public roadmap has PIX/UPI on Link this quarter; Circle’s Q2 shows stablecoin rails scaling and USDC institutional integrations growing — we ride those pipes, not reinvent them. ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com/roadmap?utm_source=openai))

Maker economics (our constraint)
- We can’t change on‑chain maker fee (always 0); the taker is 240 bps quadratic. All MM economics are off‑chain accruals. Build the dashboard and pay it fast. (Internal constraint reminder.)

Back‑of‑the‑napkin OKRs (Q3–Q4)
- Basis module live on top 25 mirrored markets; maker fill‑time p50 < 180 ms; top‑of‑book spread −20% vs baseline.
- PIX/UPI pilots to 2% of BR/IN DAUs; <5 min median cash‑in to first fill.
- Maker rebate portal live; T+1 accrual statements; dispute SLA < 24h.

Risks, read‑throughs
- Venue divergence is structural: FR regulator has shown willingness to block offshore PM; that pushes cross‑venue flow to licensed paths or VPNs — plan for both. ([anj.fr](https://anj.fr/promotion-dune-offre-de-jeux-dargent-illegale-blocage-du-site-polymarket?utm_source=openai))
- Don’t couple rails and fees: rails are distribution; vig is market microstructure. Keep them independent so we can tune each without breaking the other.
