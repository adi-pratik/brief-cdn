Title: How we outbid AMMs without maker rebates

Why this matters
- We run an off-chain CLOB with maker fee = 0 on-chain and a fixed 240 bps taker fee that scales with P·(1−P). We cannot change those on-chain. All MM economics must be off-chain. That shapes how we win depth and spreads in sports and short-horizon books.

The playbook
- Make the spread a feature: On a CLOB, the spread is a price for immediacy. Tight where stats feeds are rich and latency predictable; wider at kickoff/first pitch or on volatile sub-markets. We lean into staged quotes (pre, live, post) and publish MM guardrails per market type.
- Pay for queue, not churn: With no maker rebates on-chain, design off-chain rebates to reward time-at-best and filled resting size, not wash flow. Self-match filters and rebate audits are table stakes.
- Quote where AMMs hate inventory: AMMs socialise inventory and take basis risk on tails. We target skewed books (correct score, player props) where directional inventory is expensive for a pool and cheap for a quoting MM with limits and data.
- Keep takers honest: Our 240 bps quadratic taker makes crossing mid most expensive at 50–50, cheapest at tails. That nudges price discovery onto the book without strangling edge-taking in thin wings.
- Rails win fills: In LATAM and India, instant-pay rails turn intent into depth. Hook into PIX/UPI via partners that already localise risk/KYC and recurring flows. Stripe has pushed PIX live and previewed UPI acceptance for US businesses; design deposits so local rails feed pre‑trade balances without extra frictions. [Citations below]

What we’d ship
- MM scorecard on every market: shows time-at-best, adverse selection hit-rate, and rebate accrual to date.
- Staged-liquidity SLAs: pre‑match and live liquidity minima by market tier (A/B/C), with off‑chain rebates tied to hitting them.
- PIX/UPI deposit presets: localised, low-friction amounts that map to common stake sizes and parlay notional.

Risks
- Overpaying for ghost quotes; fix with firm‑up requirements and cancel/replace thresholds.
- Rails compliance drift; gate PIX/UPI partners behind current circulars/normatives (see sources).
