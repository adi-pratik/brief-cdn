The one thing — Stop settling on a single second
- Problem: 5m–15m crypto books that resolve on a published “last tick” invite last‑second prints and price flicks. You see it in spread behavior: makers pull, takers lunge, and adverse selection spikes right as the clock hits zero.
- Direction: Move to short TWAP (time‑weighted average price) over the final 30–60 seconds, with a median‑of‑3 or median‑of‑5 exchange price sources. This is where the market is going; Polymarket just switched its short crypto markets to TWAP earlier this month. ([poly-sniper.com](https://www.poly-sniper.com/blog/polymarket-twap-resolution-august-2026?utm_source=openai))

How to wire it (PredictStreet lens)
- Instrument: Keep the same binary up/down. Change only the resolution function: resolve to 1 if Pclose_TWAP > Pref, else 0 (or ≥ / > per spec). Window = last 30s for 5m books, 60s for 15m/4h.
- Oracle: For each second in window, pull mid (best bid+ask)/2 from at least 3 venues on a fixed panel; compute per‑venue second‑TWAP, then take cross‑venue median each second; finally average those seconds. If any venue drops >20% of seconds, drop that feed for the window (document this).
- Voids: If panel coverage <70% of seconds or two venues deviate >200 bps from panel median for >30% of window, declare void. Publish these thresholds in rules, not in support threads.
- Fees and MM: Our taker is fixed at 240 bps quadratic in P·(1−P); maker is 0 on‑chain; MM rebates are off‑chain only. TWAP reduces last‑second picks against resting liquidity, so expect tighter spreads in the final minute and lower MM rebate burn for that cohort. Calibrate off‑chain rebates to inventory at the wings (0.10–0.20 and 0.80–0.90) where selection risk remains. (Our fee constraint still binds.)

Risk and comms
- Publish the exact window and venue list. Do not move it intra‑market. Add a banner on each book with “Resolves to the average price over the final 30/60s; last‑print snipes don’t decide settlement.”
- Backtest on archived ticks to quantify: expected value change for a random taker vs snapshot; MM PnL variance in final minute; void‑rate sensitivity.

Why now
- Short‑horizon crypto is growing, and TWAP‑style resolution is quickly becoming table stakes for fair settlement.
