## Gibraltar prediction‑markets regulation live
Gibraltar published and activated the world’s first standalone Prediction Market Regulations (LN 176 of 2026) on 13 July 2026. They separate prediction‑market authorisation from gambling licences, define permissible contract types, liquidity rules, exposure and cooling‑off controls, and allow stablecoins in markets. Authorisations are expected within six months unless delayed. ([company.gi](https://company.gi/blog/gibraltar-prediction-market-regulations-2026?utm_source=openai))

### Why it matters to us
This gives us a clean regulatory lens for our ADI CLOB in Gibraltar—no more shoehorning into betting licence logic. We can now align our off‑chain rebates, 240 bps quadratic taker fee, USDC vaults, and contract approval flows with a purpose‑built regime.
