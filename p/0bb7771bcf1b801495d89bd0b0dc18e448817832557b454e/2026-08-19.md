### 19 Aug — Gibraltar codifies prediction‑market licence regime

**What moved**
- *Gibraltar introduces dedicated prediction‑market regulation.* On 13 July 2026, the jurisdiction published its Prediction Market Regulations 2026 (Legal Notice 176 of 2026), creating the world’s first standalone rulebook for prediction markets. The framework treats prediction‑market activity as distinct from betting, gaming or lotteries, allowing operators to apply via a specific authorisation—no general gambling licence required. Existing gambling licence holders must seek separate approval; the authority must process complete applications within six months unless exceptional circumstances apply. ([company.gi](https://company.gi/blog/gibraltar-prediction-market-regulations-2026?utm_source=openai))
- *Two licences already granted under new regime.* Media notes that two operators received licences immediately following the framework’s release, and Predict Street was licensed earlier in the year under the previous Act. ([igamingbusiness.com](https://igamingbusiness.com/prediction-markets/gibraltar-unveils-dedicated-prediction-markets-regulation/?utm_source=openai))

**Competitive**
