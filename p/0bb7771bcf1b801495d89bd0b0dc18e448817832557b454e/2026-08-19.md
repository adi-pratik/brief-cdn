### 14 Aug — Gibraltar grants prediction‑market licence in record time  
**What moved**  
- Gibraltar issued a prediction‑market licence under the new Gambling Act 2025 in record timing, enabling operations before full enactment.([gibraltar.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/press-releases/minister-feetham-speaking-notes-for-the-gambling-amendment-bill-2026-2362026-11857?utm_source=openai))  
- Tanzania’s Gaming Board signed an MoU with the PMO for enhanced coordination and compliance in gaming regulation.([gamingboard.go.tz](https://gamingboard.go.tz/index.php/news/gbt-and-pmo-tamisemi-sign-memorandum-of-understanding-3?utm_source=openai))  
- Prediction‑market trading volumes hit a monthly high in July: $50.6 bn total, with Polymarket US up 54%, Kalshi dominating with ~$37.7 bn.([reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/aboutcryptoindustry/comments/1vebwmy/prediction_markets_set_new_monthly_record_with/?utm_source=openai))  
**Why it matters to PredictStreet**  
- Gibraltar licence = smoother regulatory path for our ADI‑CLOB product; regulatory clarity compresses uncertainty spread.  
- Tanzania MoU = early compliance infrastructure in East Africa; potential launch region.  
- Volume surge validates product demand, liquidity depth, and competitor dynamics—spot for our fixed fee model.  
**Competitors**  
- Kalshi: volume powerhouse in regulated US.  
- Polymarket US: high growth but variable quadratic fee profile.  
**Non‑legal story**  
July’s volume record across prediction markets underscores global appetite—liquidity is the new headline.
