Title: Maker math beats megaphones

The idea
- In a CLOB, depth is a function of (a) maker cost of capital, (b) adverse selection, and (c) how often quotes get picked off before they can cancel. Our constraint: taker 240 bps quadratic in P·(1–P), maker 0 bps on-chain, with all maker economics off-chain (rebates). That’s a feature if we use it.

What to ship next (fast)
1) Targeted maker rebates by book and time of day
   - Pay for displayed size inside a protection band around mid, not just filled volume. Rewarding resting depth where spreads are widest buys us more depth-per-dollar than fill-only rebates. This mirrors fee-curve tactics competitors disclose in filings without copying their exact coefficients.
   - Guardrail: exclude self-match and same-master accounts from rebate eligibility. Label wash explicitly in ops dashboards.

2) Quote protection bands + minimum quote life
   - Small “no-take” micro-bands for 50–100ms after a fresh post on specific tickers (e.g., short-horizon crypto, in-play tennis) reduce pick-off risk without turning us into last-look FX. Apply only to designated fast books with public schedules. Publish the microstructure so quants can adapt.

3) Cross-venue basis monitor in the UI
   - Show live basis vs Polymarket and SX on matched slugs. If our best bid trails after fees, highlight to makers; if we lead, nudge takers. Basis that persists is either rules, oracle, or fees — surface which. This is already in ps-onchain-analytics; expose a trimmed version to power users.

4) Maker SLA + post-only gateway
   - Dedicated maker order path with jitter caps and priority on cancels (not fills). Publish a simple SLA (p50/p95 insert/cancel latency targets). Offer “post-only” as default for MMs to prevent crossing by mistake on mobile.

5) Result-source hygiene and void logic signposting
   - For sport/economics books, show the primary print (feed or official stat) and the challenge window in-line. Voids are product decisions; say them out loud.

Why this wins
- Kalshi and Polymarket US disclose fee curves and maintenance windows; Coinbase Predicts runs a retail UX that blends products. Our edge is a sports-first CLOB with 0 bps maker, tight rails (USDC), and off-chain rebates we can aim with a laser. If we make it cheaper to be inventory on PredictStreet between 1–5 ticks wide, depth shows up — and stays.

Footnotes (context only)
- Competitor fee curves and maintenance practices are public; virtual-asset rulebooks in ADGM/MiCA keep changing, so don’t hard-code anything that would break with a venue or rails tweak.
