19 Aug — Mechanism brief: how we buy spread without a fee switch

Why you’re reading this
- The window (Aug 17 21:24 +04 → Aug 19 09:24 +04) produced no material, cite‑worthy moves in our P0 geos or Tier‑1 competitors. Rather than print quiet lines, here’s a 2–3 minute differentiator we can ship.

The constraint
- PredictStreet is a Gibraltar betting intermediary (GLA 167) with a CLOB. Taker fee is a fixed 240 bps, quadratic in P·(1−P). Maker fee on‑chain is always 0. All MM economics must be off‑chain accruals.

The objective
- Buy tighter spreads and more displayed depth (at top‑2 price levels) without touching on‑chain fees.

What we ship (sketch)
1) Off‑chain maker rebates, tiered by true add liquidity
   - Pay only for non‑self, non‑wash, resting‑time>Δ, and price‑improving adds; haircut same‑second cancels. Tie tiers to queue position share and displayed time‑at‑best. 
2) QoS gates for rebate eligibility
   - Minimum quote‑life, max mid‑cross ratio, and quote‑to‑trade conversion. Fall below → earn base 0. 
3) Inventory bands per market (MM portal)
   - Publish target inventory ranges and sized step‑rebates when MMs lean into our unwanted side. That’s us subsidising our inventory transfer, not a blind volume race.
4) Microstructure knobs for adverse selection
   - Maker‑friendly TIFs, immediate cancel/replace credit, and small randomised match jitter on last‑millisecond sweeps in short‑horizon crypto books (to disincentivise latency snipes without "last look").
5) Cross‑venue basis monitor (front‑of‑house)
   - Show Polymarket/Limitless vs PS mid, spread, and rules deltas beside each market. Persistent basis flags either rules or fee differences — surface it to attract natural makers.

Why this wins vs AMMs
- AMMs socialise inventory and fund backstops (e.g., safebox‑style buffers) from protocol fees; CLOBs can buy the same resiliency with targeted rebates and QoS — cheaper than blanket taker cuts and without pool drag.
