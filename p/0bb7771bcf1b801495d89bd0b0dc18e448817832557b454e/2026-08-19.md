Topic: Tight spreads without maker rebates

- Problem: On a fully collateralised CLOB with a fixed 240 bps quadratic taker fee and 0 bps on‑chain maker fee, spreads widen when depth is thin and adverse selection is high. We can’t bribe the book with on‑chain rebates; all MM economics must accrue off‑chain.
- Goal: Keep two‑sided quotes close enough that price discovery happens here (not on a rival AMM) while protecting makers from being the house on stale prints.
- Levers we control:
  1) Off‑chain MM programs that pay for time‑at‑touch, not wash. Eligibility excludes self‑match and mirrored master accounts; tiers scale with displayed size and minimum presence across the curve.
  2) Inventory tools: quote groups across mutually exclusive outcomes (e.g., 1X2, player props ladders) so makers target book P&L, not single legs.
  3) TIF hygiene: good‑til‑time and IOC limits tuned per market half‑life (soccer vs 5‑minute crypto runs). Makers aren’t charged extra to cancel/replace inside our off‑chain pipes.
  4) Oracle cadence and voids: publish resolution sources and challenge windows up front; reduce discretionary voids that make makers price governance risk.
  5) Micro‑expiry markets: more short‑dated inventory where information decays fast and carry is visible, so makers can recycle margin quickly.
- How we’ll measure: cross‑venue basis (mid vs Polymarket‑compatible books), inside‑5 price improvement, and maker quote‑life vs fill ratio.
