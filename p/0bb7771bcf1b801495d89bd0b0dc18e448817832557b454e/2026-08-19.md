Title: How we win short-horizon without maker rebates

What this is
- A 2–3 minute mechanism note for PredictStreet: designing 5m/30m/1h rolling markets on our CLOB without blowing spread or MM PnL.

The constraint
- Fees: 240 bps quadratic taker, maker 0 on-chain; all MM economics are off-chain accruals. We cannot do on-chain maker rebates or fee holidays.

The problem
- Short-horizon books (crypto up/down in 5–60 minutes) create violent inventory swings and brutal adverse selection at the turn. If makers can’t price last look or charge spread for information, they widen or leave. If taker cost is fixed, aggressive flow won’t cross unless depth is real.

Mechanisms that fit our box
- Inventory bands per series: publish target inventory per leg and throttle maker size when outside the band; reopen as we re-hedge.
- Post-only + maker-protect TIF: default maker orders to post-only; auto-cancel if they’d cross after micro-moves to avoid self-hits and information leakage.
- Rolling series, one book: list a continuous ladder (e.g., 5m starting each minute) but settle all through a single oracle cadence per tenor. Makers recycle inventory; takers see a simple rail.
- Turn protection at the fix: widen or switch to auction 5–10s before each fix; match at a single price. Cuts pick-off risk while keeping taker UX predictable.
- Off-chain MM rebates tied to quote quality: pay for time-at-touch and depth (not gross notional). Disallow self-match; exclude cross-venue wash.

UX so it trades
- Show the spread and depth in native language: “Cross 51.2% to buy YES, 14k depth; 0.24% fee scaled by P(1–P).”
- One-tap roll: after settlement, auto-stage next-tenor order at user’s prior size with price guard.
- Clear voids: short-horizon void rules are product, not an afterthought; publish them in-book.

So-what
- We can list rolling short-horizon today without touching fees, and we can make makers whole with off-chain accruals that reward quote quality, not wash.

Next steps (internal)
- Prototype maker portal metrics: time-at-touch, queue position, inventory PnL by tenor.
- Ship maker-protect TIF + pre-fix micro-auction.
- Draft MM accrual schedule (C1): pay for protected depth, cap per series.
