**Gibraltar**
- Gibraltar published its **Prediction Market Regulations 2026** (Legal Notice 176 of 2026) in the Gazette on **13 July 2026**; they came into force the same day, establishing the world’s first rules crafted specifically for prediction markets rather than wedged into betting laws ([company.gi](https://company.gi/blog/gibraltar-prediction-market-regulations-2026?utm_source=openai)).
- Within that framework, the government granted a **prediction‑market licence** at record speed under the existing Act, even before the new law fully came into force, enabling an operator to trade event‑contract markets in Gibraltar ([gibraltar.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/press-releases/minister-feetham-speaking-notes-for-the-gambling-amendment-bill-2026-2362026-11857?utm_source=openai)).

**UAE / MENA**
- The Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) continues its licensing momentum: **ARP Digital obtained a VARA broker‑dealer licence** in Dubai (allowing regulated digital‑asset and stablecoin‑dirham conversions) — a forward signal within the regulated VASP ecosystem ([reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/RWATimes/comments/1vlpr6w/arp_digital_secures_dubai_vara_brokerdealer/?utm_source=openai)).
- Meanwhile, prediction markets remain in regulatory limbo in the UAE: they are neither licensed by the General Commercial Gaming Regulatory Authority (GCGRA) nor covered under VARA’s virtual‑asset regime, leaving them technically unauthorised as of April 2026 ([predictionmarketsindex.com](https://predictionmarketsindex.com/legality/united-arab-emirates/?utm_source=openai)).
