Private desk — Head of Products

Tonight’s concept (null issue):
- Why a thin news night is the best time to tighten how we pay for liquidity. Our production constraints: 240 bps quadratic taker, maker 0 on-chain, all MM economics off-chain. Our goal: narrower spreads and deeper top-of-book in soccer/tennis/cricket without lighting cash on fire.

What we’ll change (proposal):
1) Maker pay schedule that scales with useful risk, not churn
   - Pay on time-at-touch and filled notional while at touch, not gross turns.
   - Bonus for quotes that survive X seconds around state changes (goals, points, wickets) to offset adverse selection.
   - Cap rebate on same-master self-matches; wash/farm exclusion is table stakes.

2) Quote hygiene and queue protection on our CLOB
   - Minimum quote life (MQT) per market class (e.g., 150–300 ms soccer pre-match; 500–800 ms in-play) to stop flicker spam.
   - Last-look is off the table (we’re an intermediary), but we can offer Cancel-on-Event and Cancel-on-Pause flags that don’t nuke the whole book.

3) Price ladder and tick policy by instrument
   - Wider ticks when spreads are ultra-tight to avoid quote collisions (e.g., 1–2 ticks at 49–51%), finer ticks out in the tails where our quadratic fee is lowest.
   - Publish ladders per class so external MMs can pre-bake quoting.

4) Rolling short-horizon markets — a different beast
   - 5m/30m/1h “up/down” books take different inventory than match result. Pay more for persistence across the window, not just being first.
   - Oracle cadence: guarantee a deterministic resolution feed and a brief challenge window; void rules aren’t an afterthought.

Cross-venue basis (how we’ll use it):
- Build a nightly bot to monitor basis versus offshore AMMs/CLOBs. If a basis persists, tag it as: liquidity gap, rules delta, or fee delta — then fix the right one.

What success looks like:
- Pre-match soccer top-of-book narrows by 15–30 bps; in-play holds shape after goal states; lower cancel/fill ratios; fewer stale top quotes.

Ask from HoP:
- Green-light a two-week sandbox: one soccer league (pre + in-play), one tennis 250, one cricket series. Budget: capped off-chain rebates tied to the schedule above. KPI review at day 14.
