## Product & tech (short differentiator)
- Gasless vs. our rails: Gasless feels magical at checkout but hides costs in the back end. Our constraint is 240 bps quadratic taker, maker 0 on-chain; rebates are off‑chain. If we ever abstract gas on deposits/settlement, keep two hard lines: (1) show users total cost incl. any gas‑sponsor, (2) never let “free gas” steer fills toward house‑favored venues. Tie this to clear void/resolution copy on live football (added time/postponements) to win trust in Sunday peaks.
