21 Aug — What actually moved

- UK/Gibraltar — UKGC settles with QuinnBet (Gibraltar) Limited (£609,104) for AML and safer‑gambling failures (examples include 4,800 bets in a day not flagged; SoF gaps). Published 20 Aug. Why it matters: it’s the regulator we sit under for GBR access and a near‑neighbor to GLA‑167; messaging and controls are getting stricter on velocity, 18–24s, and source‑of‑funds. ([gamblingcommission.gov.uk](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/quinnbet-gibraltar-limited-to-pay-gbp609-104-for-regulatory-failures))

- Brazil — SPA (Ministry of Finance) published a 60‑month technical cooperation agreement with the International Betting Integrity Association (ACT 38/2024) to share suspicious‑bet alerts, quarterly trends, and run joint training. It specifies data fields and a five‑day alert SLA to SPA. Why it matters: the integrity pipe is formalizing — good for licensed books, bad for gray. Our sports‑resolution posture and watchlists should mirror IBIA patterns. ([gov.br](https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/composicao/orgaos/secretaria-de-premios-e-apostas/acordos-de-cooperacao/PDF19995.004919_202421ACT38.pdf))

- US — Washington State injunction against Kalshi reached its Aug 19 geofence milestone; users in‑state reported blocks on Aug 20. The order requires IP/residency geofence by Aug 19 and a multi‑source solution (e.g., GeoComply) by Sept 2. Why it matters: state AGs are asserting gambling jurisdiction even over CFTC DCMs; geofencing and product carve‑outs are live operational risks for any US‑adjacent distribution. ([agportal-s3bucket.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com](https://agportal-s3bucket.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Consumer%20Protection/64_OrdGrantAmendMtnPI.pdf?VersionId=a9FvcaBwWpQETWxbKUdHL1jqjTOD6XAi&utm_source=openai))

Mechanism note — tonight’s differentiator

- Integrity rails go live before liquidity does. SPA‑IBIA gives regulators structured alerts (who, what market, where the accounts sat), plus a five‑day handoff rule. For a CLOB like ours: bake alerting hooks into the oracle and surveillance layer now (velocity spikes, correlated fills, venue‑hops). Then pay for spread‑tightening with off‑chain MM rebates in LATAM where PIX makes fast bankroll churn cheap. That’s how you win the first hour after a rule change without widening the vig.
