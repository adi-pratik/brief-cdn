Title: The maker play when the fee is fixed

What changed for us
- Window was light on material actions in our P0 geos and competitors. Publishing a concept issue per charter.

Strategy note — Building real depth with a fixed taker and 0 on-chain maker
- Fee reality: Our taker fee is 240 bps (quadratic by P·(1–P)), maker fee 0 on-chain. All MM economics must be off-chain.
- The goal: tighter spreads + thicker size at the top of book without lighting money on fire.

Five moves to execute now
1) Maker credits that behave like negative taker for top-of-book quotes
   - Off-chain accrual that nets against future taker spend, paid only on filled maker size inside a posted spread band.
   - Guardrails: no self-match, no wash; quotes must rest a minimum time-in-force.

2) Laddered rebate bands tied to displayed depth, not volume
   - Pay more for the second and third levels (size at ±2–4 ticks), because that kills slippage for real users.
   - Turn off the faucet the moment spreads widen beyond band.

3) Cross-venue basis capture with explicit rules matching
   - Run a bot to flag >1.5% persistent basis vs Polymarket/SX for the same condition slug.
   - Before paying, confirm rules/oracle parity; if void rules differ, treat as separate instruments.

4) Short-horizon books as inventory sinks
   - 5m/30m/1h crypto up/down markets drain directional risk from the sports book; rebate makers for delta-hedging across both.

5) Rails that actually settle for MMs in LATAM/India/APAC hours
   - Pre-approved USDC lines + instant off-ramp playbooks (PIX/UPI/SEPA Instant) for market makers so they can recycle capital intra-session.

Risk controls (do these or don’t do the program)
- Adverse-selection filter: no rebates on trades executed within X seconds of a regime-shift print (goal updates, red cards, official stats, CPI/BLS release windows).
- Wash and common-control screens: master-account checks; deny payouts on self-cross or same-beneficial-owner activity.
- Circuit rails: widen bands and cut rebates automatically during oracle delays or data-feed anomalies.

Why it matters (inference)
- We can’t change on-chain fees, but we can buy displayed liquidity precisely where users feel it: tighter spreads, more size, less slippage. The house doesn’t win here — our venue wins recurring order flow because it’s cheaper to trade.

What to ship
- Product: maker dashboard (targets, bands, earned credits, clawbacks), plus venue-wide ‘depth score’ badge on markets.
- Ops: weekly audit of rebate-eligible flow; publish anonymized scorecards to keep MMs honest.
- Data: cross-venue basis monitor in the internal analytics, piped to the maker portal as alerts.
