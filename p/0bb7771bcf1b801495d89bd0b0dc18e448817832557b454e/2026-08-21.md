Null issue — concept (2–3 minutes)

Title: Tight spreads without being the house

Why this now
- We’re lining up more short‑horizon sports markets (goals, corners, overs in 10–15m windows) for World Cup–adjacent traffic. Getting price + depth without AMM inventory is the trick.

Mechanism — the playbook
- Stay CLOB: We don’t warehouse risk. Makers quote; takers pay the vig. Our taker fee is a fixed 240 bps, quadratic in P×(1−P); maker fee on‑chain is 0; MM economics are off‑chain rebates.
- Maker bounties (off‑chain): Pay for quote quality, not just volume — tight, two‑sided, near‑mid with minimum time‑at‑touch. Daily cash accrual, per‑market ladders.
- Rolling market design: Staggered 5m/10m/15m windows with clear cut‑off and no late fills; per‑window order books avoid stale inventory traps.
- Resolution/voids: Stats Perform primary, tiny challenge window; pre‑declare edge cases (VAR resets, abandoned matches) to prevent discretionary voids.
- Pricing hygiene: Enforce min tick, min size, and cancel‑replace throttles to reduce flicker and last‑look behavior without freezing the book.

Why this wins vs peers
- AMM pools (e.g., Azuro architecture) monetize spread via margin and reinforcement, but the pool eats adverse selection during in‑play; our CLOB pushes that to makers who are paid to manage it. ([gem.azuro.org](https://gem.azuro.org/hub/apps/APIs?utm_source=openai))
- Polymarket demonstrates fee‑funded maker programs and fee curves symmetric around 50% — same math we already run, but we keep makers’ on‑chain fee at zero and tune rebates off‑chain per sport. ([help.polymarket.com](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364478-trading-fees?utm_source=openai))
- DCMs (Kalshi) are a different wrapper entirely; do not chase their listing cadence with our betting‑intermediary UX. ([cftc.gov](https://www.cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/IndustryFilings/TradingOrganizationProducts?Organization=KEX&col=Remarks&dir=DESC&page=0&utm_source=openai))

Next steps (internal)
- Pilot “crossed‑spread bounty” on 3 live soccer micro‑books for 7 days; pay only when both sides quoted within X ticks for Y cumulative seconds.
- Ship per‑window books + pre‑baked VAR/abandonment rules into templates.
- Add maker telemetry to the MM portal: time‑at‑touch, queue position, adverse‑selection PnL.
