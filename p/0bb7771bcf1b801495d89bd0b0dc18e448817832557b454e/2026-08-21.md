### The maker‑rebate ladder (off‑chain)
Pay rebates weekly in USDC with three ingredients:
- Time‑at‑top‑of‑book (TTB): percent of seconds at best bid/ask with ≥$X displayed size, per market family. Reward quote quality, not spam.
- Adverse‑selection guard: haircut rebate when maker PnL < −Y bps versus mid; boost when they internalize toxic flow and still keep spread tight.
- Inventory bands: pay a higher tier when the net inventory sigma stays within band B over the week; penalize constant “cliff” exposure that turns our book into someone else’s option. 

How it pays out
- Tier A (Core): TTB ≥ 55% and avg. posted depth ≥ $2k → 40–60 bps rebate on filled maker notional.
- Tier B (Stretch): TTB ≥ 70% and depth ≥ $5k → 60–85 bps.
- Tier C (Target): TTB ≥ 80% and depth ≥ $10k with low adverse selection → up to 100 bps.
- No on‑chain fee changes; pure off‑chain accruals consistent with our constraints.
