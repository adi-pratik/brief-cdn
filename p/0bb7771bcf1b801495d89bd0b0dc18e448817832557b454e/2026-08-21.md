Concept — Buying the spread without touching on‑chain fees

What we have
- Fixed taker fee: 240 bps, quadratic in P·(1–P). Maker fee on‑chain: 0. All maker economics must be paid off‑chain.

What that means
- We can’t do a public maker rebate on‑chain like a DEX or a US DCM tweak. We have to aim the hose off‑chain.

How to win anyway (actionable)
- Segment the book by adverse‑selection risk and rebate where we need depth, not where we already have it.
- Pay for “useful” liquidity only: tight quotes at size that actually get hit, not parked icebergs off the market.
- Tie rebates to fill‑quality and inventory risk: size‑ and time‑weighted, symmetric around 50% so we buy both wings when the fee’s highest.
- Introduce event windows: elevated rebates for first 10 minutes after a listing, last 15 before close, and during live‑play spikes.

Why now
- Competitors publicly reward makers and/or vary taker fees by product; we must do it privately and precisely. See their schedules for reference. ([docs.polymarket.com](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees?utm_source=openai))

Guardrails
- No rebate on self‑match or wash; exclude master‑account cross. Kill lists, velocity caps, and off‑venue hedging attestations. (Our “wash, self‑match, rebate farming” lens.)

Metric to watch
- 30‑day median NBBO spread vs. cross‑venue basis on mapped markets; depth at 1% slippage. If spread narrows and basis collapses toward fee‑parity, the hose is pointed right.

What to ship
- A/B two rebate formulas in production for 14 days: (A) fill‑weighted only; (B) fill‑ plus time‑at‑touch. Keep total spend flat; pick the one that buys more inside‑touch fills per dollar.
