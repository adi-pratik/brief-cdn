- Nigeria — SEC exposes draft crypto-market rulebook (Aug 20). Nigeria’s SEC published draft “Proposed Rules – Digital and Virtual Assets Operations, Custody and Markets,” covering issuance, tokenisation, trading venues, custody and advisory, with extraterritorial reach to anyone serving Nigerian users. Comments due two weeks from exposure. ([home.sec.gov.ng](https://home.sec.gov.ng/for-investors/proposed-rules-digital-and-virtual-assets-operations-custody-and-markets/?utm_source=openai))
  - Why it matters to PredictStreet (inference): Our TZ–EA play books next to West Africa. If Ghana/NG rails open for USDC on/off, we’ll want clarity on whether a Gibraltar-licensed betting intermediary listing non-sports binaries to Nigerian residents sits inside these rules (likely yes under “trading, custody, transfer and settlement”). Workstreams: Licensing, Trust, Rails.

Same day, its sanctions feed surfaced prior action context against Hillside/bet365 (consumer-protection failures) — useful read-through on enforcement stance. ([kansspelautoriteit.nl](https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/nieuwe-inzichten-gokgedrag-minderjarigen-en-jongvolwassenen?utm_source=openai))
