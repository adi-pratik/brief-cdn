Strategy note (tonight’s deep cut)

- If the play is speed + inventory, our CLOB must welcome makers while defending takers from slippage. With a fixed 240 bps quadratic taker fee and 0 bps maker on‑chain (and all MM economics handled off‑chain), any rival’s public maker rebate or embedded AMM subsidy will show up as persistent basis. The answer isn’t copy/paste rebates we can’t post on‑chain; it’s (a) post‑only windows around restarts, (b) maker scorecards that pay for displayed depth at the touch, and (c) void/resolution rules that avoid “operator decides” tail‑risk. See the podcast for the how.
